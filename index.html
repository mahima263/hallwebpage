Greater Than Us · Foundation: The Ground We Build On

Strengthening the Foundation of IIT Kanpur

Foundation is the first strategic priority of Greater Than Us, strengthening the essential infrastructure behind the Institute’s growth. Today, its most pressing need is student housing.

The home we knew is running out of room

  • No government funding

    With no government funds for new halls, they can only be built with the support of alumni and well-wishers.

  • Overcrowded rooms

    Enrolment has outgrown our halls. Rooms designed for one or two students now house three.

  • Academics affected

    Less quiet and less space make it harder to study and to take part in campus life.

  • Well-being at risk

    Cramped rooms and few common spaces can lead to stress and isolation.

Every student deserves what we had: a place to belong.

976women students will call Hall 15 home

From One Generation to the Next

Hall 15 shows what we can do together

Hall 15, IIT Kanpur’s new residential hall for women, is nearing completion, made possible by the support of our alumni. It is a testament to what this community can achieve when it comes together for its students.

Hall 15 is the beginning. Halls 16, 17 and 18 are next.

Building for the Future: Halls 16, 17 & 18

Three new halls. Home to 3,000+ students.

Designed around comfort, community living and sustainability, Halls 16, 17 and 18 will give future IITians a place to learn, connect and thrive.

Solar energy, rainwater harvesting, water reuse, natural light and green landscaping are built into the design, making the halls efficient and future-ready.

Key Amenities

Built for the way students live today
