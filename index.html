A Legacy Built to Last

Halls 1 and 2 have stood the test of time, becoming an enduring part of the IIT Kanpur experience.

They remind us that a hall of residence is more than a structure. It is a community, a shared experience and a home that stays with you long after graduation.

With Halls 16, 17 and 18, we carry this spirit forward, building spaces that will serve and inspire generations of IITians for decades to come.

Building for a Growing IIT Kanpur

As IIT Kanpur continues to grow, so does the need for modern residential infrastructure that supports a vibrant campus life.

Halls 16, 17 and 18 will provide:

  • Contemporary residential facilities for students.
  • Spaces that foster community and collaboration.
  • Essential amenities for a fulfilling residential experience.

This is an investment in the IITK experience and the generations who will shape its future.

Key Features

  • Centralised AC rooms, ergonomic furniture, personal storage
  • High-speed Wi-Fi throughout the hall of residence
  • Dining & Canteen Facilities
  • Laundry facilities with drying rooms
  • Study Rooms & Workspaces for quiet academic focus
  • Recreation Areas
  • Smart Lounges with social & collaborative spaces
  • Guest Rooms for parents and visitors
  • Fire & Safety Compliance
  • Accessibility-friendly design

From One Generation to the Next:The Story of Hall 15

The success of Hall 15, IIT Kanpur’s residential hall for women, is a testament to what alumni support can achieve.

Now nearing completion, Hall 15 reflects a shared commitment to creating better residential opportunities for students.

With Halls 16, 17 and 18, the journey continues. And you can be a part of it.
